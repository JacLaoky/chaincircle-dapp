Every transaction is recorded by the Flask backend and then independently verified against Sepolia via JSON-RPC.

1

Create or join

The creator sets the contribution, number of members and round length. Each member locks the same collateral to join.

2

Circle starts

When the circle is full it starts automatically. Payout order = join order and is fixed on-chain.

3

Contribute each round

Every member pays the contribution before the deadline. The last payment automatically sends the whole pot to that round's recipient.

4

Defaults are covered

If someone doesn't pay, anyone can settle the round after the deadline. The missing amount is taken from the defaulter's collateral.

5

Withdraw

Pots and returned collateral are credited to your balance. Click Withdraw to move them to your wallet (pull-payment pattern).

6

Build reputation

On-time payments, defaults and completed circles are stored on-chain — a portable credit history for the unbanked.
